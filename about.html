Service

Renovation & Design

We specialize in high-end residential renovations that transform outdated houses into modern masterpieces. Our team understands that renovating a home is a deeply personal journey, and we are here to guide you through every step.

Our renovation services include full-scale interior remodeling, structural improvements, and bespoke kitchen and bath design. We focus on maximizing space and improving the flow of your home while preserving its original character where it matters most.

Whether it's a traditional Japanese house needing a modern touch or a contemporary apartment requiring more soul, we provide tailored solutions that fit your needs. We manage everything from planning and permitting to construction oversight.

Let us help you breathe new life into your existing space. We combine traditional craftsmanship with innovative technology to deliver results that exceed expectations.
