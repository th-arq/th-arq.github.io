U.S. EMBASSY PORT MORESBY

Location: Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea

Size: 18,837 SM

Client: U.S. Department of State, OBO

The Port Moresby compound lies within Harbour City, a new mixed-use development of diplomatic, commercial, and high-end residential uses on land reclaimed from the Coral Sea on a man-made peninsula. The vision for the compound is a marine theme inspired by the coastal region of Papua New Guinea and its maritime culture. Two distinct zones - a representational front lawn and entry plaza; and a rear recreation area - support the complementary functions of diplomacy and community.

Six buildings occupy the campus including a 6,369 square meter New Office Building, Service Building, Compound Access Pavilion Facilities, a Warehouse and Shops building, MSGR and recreational facilities. The compound is nearly self-sufficient pursuing LEED Gold certification for New Construction with on-site rainwater collection and treatment, as well as solar power generation. The design is largely a climate and place-driven solution. It is defined by its civic quality and approach to sustainability and human comfort.
