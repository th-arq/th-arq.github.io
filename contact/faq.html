FAQ

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve the Los Angeles metropolitan area, including Santa Monica, Beverly Hills, and Pasadena. For larger commercial projects, we are open to discussing opportunities throughout California.

How long does a typical renovation project take?

The timeline varies depending on the scope. A kitchen or bath remodel usually takes 4–8 weeks, while a full house renovation can take 4–10 months, including the design and permitting phase.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes, we provide an initial consultation and a rough estimate at no cost. For detailed project bidding and architectural planning, a design fee may apply.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, TAG Inc. is a fully licensed general contractor in the State of California (#123456) and carries comprehensive liability insurance.
